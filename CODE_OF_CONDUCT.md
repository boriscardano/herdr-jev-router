# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, safe, and respectful project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus disagreement on ideas, evidence, and project outcomes.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and
take proportionate action. Actions may include a warning, removal of content,
temporary exclusion, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves when
necessary. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern is prohibited.
